Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2025

1. Introduction

The app Quiet Pomodoro is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a user. The app is developed and maintained by Adam Pukaluk.

2. Data Collected and Storage

The app Quiet Pomodoro collects and stores only essential data needed for its functionality:

  • Session settings: session duration, theme preference, vibration settings, sound settings, and auto-start configuration,
  • Session statistics: number of completed sessions and session duration history from recent days,
  • Timer state: current timer status for widget and snapshot functionality.

All data is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. The app does not transmit any data to remote servers or third parties.

3. Data Usage

Collected data is used exclusively for:

  • personalizing your Pomodoro experience (theme, notifications, sounds, timer duration),
  • tracking session statistics to help you monitor your productivity,
  • displaying your session state in the app and system widget.

4. Notifications and Sounds

The app may send local notifications on your device when a Pomodoro session phase ends. The app also plays short notification sounds within the application. These features require system permissions granted by you through both your device's system settings and the app's settings.

Important: Notifications and sounds do not contain any personal data and are processed entirely on your device.

5. System Widget

The timer state is stored in the same local storage and may be read by your system's widget functionality. This data never leaves your device.

6. System Permissions

The app requires the following permissions:

  • Notifications (optional): to send session reminders and phase completion alerts. You control this permission through your device settings and app settings,
  • Audio playback: to play notification sounds within the app.

The app does not require or request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or any other sensitive data.

7. External Libraries and Services

The app uses the following technologies:

  • Expo SDK: for cross-platform development (modules: expo-notifications, expo-av, and others for core functionality),
  • React Native: for application framework.

These libraries operate entirely within the app and do not collect data. Your data is not shared with any third parties through these libraries.

8. Remote Analytics

The app currently does not use remote analytics or telemetry. All data remains on your device. If remote analytics is implemented in future versions, this policy will be updated and you will be notified.

9. Data Sharing

Your data is never shared with external companies, advertisers, or analytics services. The app does not contain advertising or third-party tracking services that collect your data.

10. Children and Data Protection

This app is not intentionally directed to children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately. The app complies with applicable data protection regulations including GDPR and CCPA.

11. User Rights and Data Deletion

You have complete control over your data:

  • You can delete all saved statistics and reset your settings at any time using the "Reset history" or "Clear all data" option in the app settings,
  • You can disable notifications and sounds through the app settings or your device settings,
  • Uninstalling the app will permanently remove all locally stored data from your device.

12. Data Security and Retention

Your data is stored securely on your device. Since all data is stored locally and not transmitted to any server, security depends on your device's security settings. You have full control over when your data is deleted by using the app's reset feature or uninstalling the app.

13. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected with an updated effective date at the top of this document. Continued use of the app following changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
